## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a high-energy, dependable, and frictionless entertainment ticketing environment. The visual direction balances the excitement of premiere cinema with the reliability of a modern transactional interface.

### Brand Identity & Personality
- **Energetic & Approachable:** Inviting visuals, high clarity, and open spatial arrangements turn movie selection and checkout into an effortless, joyful activity.
- **Trustworthy & Transparent:** Clear ticketing paths, explicit seat assignments, and distinct price breakdowns remove cognitive friction and ticket-buying anxiety.
- **Modern & Tactile:** Clean geometric construction accented with soft drop shadows and rounded geometries feels tactile, digital-native, and responsive across mobile and desktop interfaces.

### Target Audience
Cinephiles, social moviegoers, families, and everyday digital consumers who demand rapid screening discovery, intuitive seat selection, and immediate checkout completion.

### Design Movement
**Modern Functionalism with Ambient Softness.** The interface relies on crisp white and slate structural planes, vibrant indigo brand accents, fine slate dividers, and deep organic elevation to communicate affordance without unnecessary visual ornamentation.

## Colors

The color architecture is built around an intentional hierarchy: radiant Indigo directs attention toward active selections and primary transactions, supported by purposeful status accents for ticket lifecycle management.

### Primary & Brand Identity
- **Primary Indigo (`#4F46E5`):** The primary interaction engine. Used for main calls to action, selected seat states, active filters, and primary navigation highlights.
- **Royal Violet (`#4338CA`):** Darker interactive peer used for primary hover states, active states, and focused navigation anchors.

### Accents & Status Indicators
- **Emerald Teal (`#10B981`):** Applied exclusively to confirmed states, VIP available upgrades, payment success, and positive receipts.
- **Coral Red (`#EF4444`):** Strictly reserved for occupied/unavailable seats, destructive checkout cancellations, and validation errors.
- **Amber Orange (`#F59E0B`):** Denotes temporary reservations, expiring cart hold countdowns, and pending processing alerts.

### Neutrals & Surfaces
- **Canvas (`#F8FAFC`):** The default light slate background creating soft, glare-free contrast for theater schedules and card modules.
- **Surface Elevation (`#FFFFFF`):** Pure white container background for cards, seat charts, dialogs, and navigation headers.
- **Border Default (`#E2E8F0`):** Subtle structural divider framing cards, inputs, and category tabs.
- **Text Primary (`#0F172A`):** Deep slate providing high readability for headlines, timings, and prices.
- **Text Secondary (`#64748B`):** Mid-tone slate for movie metadata, tags, format labels, and helper copy.

## Typography

The typography pairing merges the friendly, structural geometry of **Plus Jakarta Sans** with the utilitarian precision of **Inter**.

- **Display & Headlines (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Delivers a charismatic, modern feel to movie titles, cinema screen badges, and billboard headers. Tight negative letter-spacing keeps bold titles cohesive and impactful.
- **Body & Data (Inter):** Maximizes readability across dense schedules, showtime grids, seating pricing tiers, and legal ticket terms.
- **Labels & Microcopy (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Provides clear, legible text on buttons, pill badges, and seat indicators at small sizes.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout operates on an 8pt base grid with a responsive container structure optimized for discovery grids and interactive theater layouts.

### Grid Architecture
- **Desktop (1280px+):** 12-column grid, max container width `1280px`, `gutter: 1.5rem`, `margin: 2rem`. Showtime selectors and seat plan views deploy side-by-side with sticky order summaries.
- **Tablet (768px - 1027px):** 8-column grid, `gutter: 1.25rem`, `margin: 1.5rem`. Showtime rails scroll horizontally, seating maps feature sticky bottom progress actions.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column grid, `gutter-mobile: 1rem`, `margin-mobile: 1rem`. Vertical single-column stack with persistent floating checkout bar.

### Spacing Principles
- Spacers `space-xs` and `space-sm` are strictly reserved for badge insets, icon-to-label distances, and seat grid padding.
- `space-md` acts as standard inner padding for form fields, ticket list items, and standard cards.
- `space-xl` through `space-3xl` separate top-level screening categories, hero banners, and seat stage perspectives.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system uses an ambient, colored-diffusion shadow approach rather than stark, heavy drop shadows, preserving an airy, modern feel.

### Elevation Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Flat):** Resting canvas `#F8FAFC`, baseline schedule panels, and disabled seat markers. `border: 1px solid #E2E8F0`.
- **Level 1 (Surface Cards & Showtimes):** Movie cards, date selector pills, and regular container elements.
  - Shadow: `0 1px 3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 4px 8px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`.
  - Border: `1px solid #E2E8F0`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Popovers & Sticky Rails):** Summary drawers, hover states on interactive cards, and screening filter dropdowns.
  - Shadow: `0 8px 20px -4px rgba(79, 70, 229, 0.08), 0 4px 10px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
  - Border: `1px solid rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.8)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Seating Floating Bars):** Seat zoom controls, trailer overlays, and primary mobile ticket sticky bar.
  - Shadow: `0 20px 30px -8px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12), 0 8px 12px -4px rgba(79, 70, 229, 0.06)`.

## Shapes

The shape hierarchy uses smooth, inviting curves that mirror modern physical cinema seating and mobile operating systems.

### Rounding Scale
- **Base Components (`rounded-base`, 0.5rem / 8px):** Checkboxes, seat layout nodes, timing selector chips, and micro badges.
- **Cards & Inputs (`rounded-lg`, 1rem / 16px):** Movie cards, text input fields, showtime group containers, and seat layout legends.
- **Large Panels (`rounded-xl`, 1.5rem / 24px):** Checkout summary cards, interactive seating chart viewport frames, and trailer modals.
- **Interactive Pills (`rounded-full`, 9999px):** Primary filter badges, format selectors (IMAX, 3D, VIP), search bars, and floating ticket counters.

## Components

All components are strictly instrumented with deterministic `data-testid` attributes to ensure seamless end-to-end automation with Playwright.

### 1. Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#4F46E5`, text `#FFFFFF`, hover `#4338CA`, active `#3730A3`. Corner radius `rounded-lg` (16px) or `rounded-full` for icon action buttons. Focus ring: `3px solid rgba(79, 70, 229, 0.35)`.
  - Test ID: `data-testid="button-primary-{action}"` (e.g., `data-testid="button-primary-checkout"`).
- **Secondary:** Surface `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, text `#0F172A`. Hover: background `#F8FAFC`, border `#CBD5E1`.
  - Test ID: `data-testid="button-secondary-{action}"`.
- **Destructive:** Surface `#FEF2F2`, border `1px solid #FCA5A5`, text `#EF4444`. Hover: background `#EF4444`, text `#FFFFFF`.
  - Test ID: `data-testid="button-danger-{action}"`.

### 2. Pills & Badges
- **Status Pills:** Pill geometry (`rounded-full`), padding `0.25rem 0.75rem`, label typography `label-sm`.
  - Available/Success: `#ECFDF5`, text `#065F46`, border `1px solid #A7F3D0` (`data-testid="badge-status-confirmed"`).
  - Occupied/Sold Out: `#FEF2F2`, text `#991B1B`, border `1px solid #FECACA` (`data-testid="badge-status-sold-out"`).
  - Hold/Expiring: `#FFFBEB`, text `#92400E`, border `1px solid #FDE68A` (`data-testid="badge-status-pending"`).
- **Experience Tags:** Format labels (IMAX, 4DX, Dolby Atmos) utilize `#EEF2FF`, text `#4F46E5`, border `1px solid #C7D2FE` (`data-testid="badge-tag-{format}"`).

### 3. Movie Cards & Showtime Lists
- **Movie Poster Card:** Pure white surface, Level 1 shadow, `rounded-xl` container with an inner `overflow-hidden` poster ratio of 2:3. Contains title, rating pill, runtime, and genre pills.
  - Test ID: `data-testid="card-movie-{movieId}"`.
- **Showtime Slot:** Pill-shaped selector with `#FFFFFF` background, `1px solid #E2E8F0` border, `Inter` semi-bold time copy, and subtle format sub-label. Hover converts to `#EEF2FF` border `#4F46E5`.
  - Test ID: `data-testid="slot-showtime-{showtimeId}"`.

### 4. Interactive Seating Map
- **Seat Node:** 32x32px squircle (`rounded-base`) interactive target with visible tooltip affordance.
  - Available: `#FFFFFF` border `2px solid #CBD5E1`, hover `#EEF2FF` with `#4F46E5` border.
  - Selected: `#4F46E5` background, `#FFFFFF` icon marker, glow shadow `0 0 0 3px rgba(79, 70, 229, 0.25)`.
  - Occupied: `#F1F5F9` background, `#CBD5E1` border, cursor `not-allowed`.
  - Reserved/VIP: `#ECFDF5` background with `#10B981` border.
  - Test ID: `data-testid="seat-{row}-{number}"` with dynamic `data-state="available|selected|occupied|reserved"`.

### 5. Form Inputs & Selectors
- Standard text input, date-picker, and promo code components sit on `#FFFFFF` surfaces with `1px solid #E2E8F0` borders, `rounded-lg` geometry, and inset padding of `0.75rem 1rem`.
- Focus shifts border to `#4F46E5` with an outer ring `3px rgba(79, 70, 229, 0.15)`.
- Test ID: `data-testid="input-{fieldName}"` (e.g., `data-testid="input-promo-code"`).

### 6. Booking Summary & Cart Checkout
- Sticky desktop sidebar or mobile floating bar featuring countdown timer pill (`data-testid="cart-timer"`), seat item chips with dynamic removal buttons (`data-testid="button-remove-seat-{id}"`), subtotal calculation, and high-visibility checkout button.
